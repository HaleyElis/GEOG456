Across the three aerial photographs, the playa surface remains bright and exposed, but the location and intensity of the reddish and gray mineral staining changes over time. The agricultural fields along the eastern and southern edges shift in shape and visibility, with some green parcels becoming more distinct in later images. The overall lakebed boundary and surrounding desert landscape stay similar, while the interior patterns of moisture, sediment, and salt deposits appear to move and reorganize between 2005, 2012, and 2020.
